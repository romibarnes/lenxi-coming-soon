The thing that changes everything.

Salons use a giant machine for this part. We didn't want a giant machine on your sink, so we spent nine months (yepp, like an actual baby) making a small, pretty version that does the job:

  • ✓Activates the oil: heat unlocks the actives. Cold oil = pretty bottle, not much else.
  • ✓Opens your pores: so the oil sinks in instead of sliding off.
  • ✓Wakes up blood flow: the part that actually feeds the follicle.
  • ✓Loosens the residue: buildup, dead skin, product. All of it.
